One flat table, ranked highest first. **A shipped row is deleted, not ticked**; when you close
one, put what a future session would get wrong without it into `CLAUDE.md` or `docs/`. Rows are
intentions, not specifications: a better idea beats the row, so change the row. The only hard
constraints are the canonical contracts named in `CLAUDE.md`.

| 68 | **Hands, not boxes.** Both controllers are tinted dev cubes at the grip pose, because `VRController.GetModel` returns `models/dev/box.vmdl` for every device `[SOURCE]`. The end state is the citizen's own arm and fingers on IK — `LobbyPlayer.ApplyHandPose` already drives a seated arm from `Gambit.Chess.TerryPose` through `SetIk`, which takes a world transform and needs no GameObjects (`docs/terry.md`). | 60 | **Shadows flicker in VR only.** Diagnosed by review, not yet reproduced. The census is 9 shadow-casting local lights by day and 13 by night, not four: `Light.Shadows` defaults true and `ChessRing`'s eight per-table spots never set it. Shadow maps are camera-independent and rendered once per frame for all views `[SOURCE]`, so three views do not triple the budget — but `ScreenSize` and the resolution derived from it ARE per view, and the companion's 75° FOV asks for a bigger map than the ~105° eyes. `GetOrCreateCacheEntry` only guards against a downgrade, so the companion's larger request reallocates the map and forces a re-render inside the same frame, and the eyes' smaller one does it again next frame: resolution alternates, `ComputeBiasScale` has resolution in the denominator, and the bias popping every frame is the flicker. The moving sun is not the trigger (directional goes down the CSM path, which never touches that cache); head-tracked `SnapToTexel` and per-view contact shadows are a separate VR-only source. Ladder, re-ranked: `r.shadows.debug 1` first (the only step that returns a number — maps rendered vs the budget of 8, and per-light resolutions), then `r.shadows.maxresolution 128` (pins resolution, so it is the single-variable test), then `r.shadows.contact.enabled 0`, then `gambit_vr_companion`. `r.shadows.updates 64` is predicted a no-op: it cannot stop a forced re-render. Cheapest mitigation if confirmed: `Shadows = false` on the eight `TableLight` spots, or only the seated table's. Also worth fixing either way — the companion copies the authored camera's FOV rather than deriving it from the eye projection, which is both the root of the divergence and why the monitor is framed differently from the headset. |
| 54 | **The hint copy has no VR branch.** Ten panels under `client/Code/UI/` name a key to a player holding two controllers — every wall board's "PRESS E", `InfoScreen`'s "Press E or Esc to close", the `HudHints` keycap stack — so this is wider than a string swap: it wants one place that answers "what does this control say right now", which does not exist yet. The two info boards are part of any change to how the world behaves (`CLAUDE.md`), and they are the two that must not be forgotten: `CenterInfoPanel.razor` and `InfoScreen.razor`'s Welcome branch. |
| 48 | **The music HUD is still flat in both eyes.** `LocalMusicSystem` builds its own `ScreenPanel` on a `NotSaved` object, which `VrHud.Adopt` never sees — it adopts the scene's UI object only. Either adopt every root panel in the scene or give that one a world panel of its own; the first is a rule, the second is a special case, and the choice is the row. |
| 40 | **No comfort options.** Turning is smooth only, at `VrInput.TurnRate` 110°/s off the right stick, with no snap option and no vignette, and the playspace re-anchors under the player on a seat capture. Snap turn is the one setting most VR players look for first. The settings seam exists (`BoardSettingsScreen`, `SettingsModel`), so the work is deciding what belongs there versus a console knob. |
| 30 | **Upstream: `button_b` never binds on an Index.** `game/core/cfg/fpxr/valve_knuckles.json` gives it a `paths` key holding a bare string, where every other `paths` in both shipped profiles is the per-hand object map and every single binding uses `path`. One character, `paths` → `path`, as a PR to sbox-public. Nothing here depends on it — Escape answers to A as well — and that repo's master is flaky, so verify only this. |
